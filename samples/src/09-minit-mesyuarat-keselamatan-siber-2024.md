# Minutes of the ICT Steering Committee Meeting No. 2/2024

**Reference:** JPDS/TM/JKP/2/2024
**Date:** 12 March 2024
**Time:** 10.00 am to 12.15 pm
**Venue:** Bilik Mesyuarat Utama, Level 4, Menara Digital, Kuching

## Attendees

- Dr. Jessica Lim Siew Ling, Director of Information Technology (Chairperson)
- Encik Awang Zulkifli bin Awang Bujang, ICT Security Officer (ICTSO)
- Puan Rosnah binti Mohamad, Head of Systems Development
- Encik Raymond Anak Jugah, Head of Network and Infrastructure
- Puan Lily Chong Mei Yee, Representative of Human Resources Department
- Encik Syed Ahmad Fauzi, Representative of Finance Department
- Cik Nurul Aini binti Hamdan, Secretary

Apologies: Encik Peter Anak Ngelai, Head of Data Centre (on official leave).

## Agenda

1. Confirmation of minutes of Meeting No. 1/2024
2. Matters arising
3. Cybersecurity awareness training
4. Multi-factor authentication rollout
5. Data centre backup review
6. Any other business

## 1. Confirmation of Minutes

The minutes of Meeting No. 1/2024 held on 9 January 2024 were confirmed without amendment.

## 2. Matters Arising

The ICTSO reported that the phishing simulation conducted in February 2024 sent 420 test e-mails, and 87 staff (20.7 percent) clicked the link. The Chairperson found the figure too high against the target of below 10 percent.

## 3. Cybersecurity Awareness Training

The ICTSO proposed that all staff complete the online module Cyber Hygiene Essentials, which takes about 90 minutes. The Human Resources representative noted that only 31 percent of staff had completed it so far.

**Decision 3.1:** The Committee decided that all staff must complete the Cyber Hygiene Essentials module by 30 June 2024.

**Decision 3.2:** Staff who have not completed the module by 30 June 2024 will have their access to the e-Procurement and HRMS systems suspended until completion.

**Decision 3.3:** Heads of Division will receive a weekly completion report every Monday from 1 April 2024.

## 4. Multi-Factor Authentication Rollout

The Head of Network and Infrastructure reported that multi-factor authentication (MFA) has been enabled for all administrator accounts. The Committee agreed to extend MFA to all staff e-mail accounts by 31 August 2024. The estimated cost of RM38,500 for hardware tokens was approved within the existing ICT budget.

## 5. Data Centre Backup Review

The Chairperson asked that restoration of backup be tested. The Head of Network and Infrastructure will carry out a full restoration test of the finance system on 18 May 2024 and report the results at the next meeting. Daily backups are kept for 30 days.

## 6. Any Other Business

The Finance representative asked for the training budget for the advanced security course for the ICT team. The Chairperson asked that a paper be submitted for the next meeting. Cost estimate is RM24,000 for eight (8) officers.

## Action Items

- ICTSO: send reminder e-mail on the 30 June 2024 deadline to all staff by 22 March 2024
- Human Resources: add Cyber Hygiene Essentials to the performance appraisal checklist by 30 April 2024
- Head of Network and Infrastructure: complete backup restoration test by 18 May 2024
- Head of Network and Infrastructure: deploy MFA to all e-mail accounts by 31 August 2024
- Secretary: circulate draft minutes within five (5) working days

## Next Meeting

Meeting No. 3/2024 will be held on 11 June 2024 at 10.00 am.

The meeting ended at 12.15 pm.
